The problem we solve

Most platforms don't fail at selection. They fail at execution.

Enterprise ITAM platforms — ServiceNow, Flexera, Snow, Zylo, SmartTrack — are capable tools. The gap between capability and output is almost never the platform. It's the configuration, the data architecture, the publisher tuning, and the governance model that determine whether the platform produces a trusted, defensible licence position.

We've inherited dozens of deployments that cost seven figures and still can't produce a clean compliance position three years in. The platform isn't the problem. The implementation was.

Our implementation practice is built around one discipline: getting from deployment to trusted output as directly as possible — and then ensuring your internal team can sustain it.

The platform is already selected. Now make it produce.
How we deliver

Sprint-delivered. Governance from day one.

Every engagement follows a structured methodology — two-week sprints, weekly progress reporting, clear acceptance criteria at each milestone. We don't disappear for three months and surface with a report.

What we focus on first

Most failed deployments fail at the data layer — not the platform configuration. Before writing a single recognition rule or publisher pack, we design the inventory architecture:

  • Discovery source mapping and coverage gaps
  • Inventory normalisation and deduplication logic
  • Application recognition rate benchmarking
  • Publisher-specific metering and measurement design
  • Entitlement model at the granularity the platform needs

What changes at hand-back

We design for sustainability, not dependency. Every engagement ends with a clear operating model, trained internal team, and governance cadence your people can run without us.

  • 01

    Vendor-independent assessment

    We hold no reseller agreements with any platform vendor. Every recommendation is grounded in your estate, your publishers, and your operating model — not anyone's renewal calendar.

  • 02

    Data-first architecture design

    We design the inventory and data architecture before writing a single rule. The quality of every downstream answer depends entirely on the quality of the input data.

  • 03

    Sprint delivery with clear acceptance criteria

    Two-week delivery sprints. Weekly progress reporting. Clear acceptance criteria — inventory coverage, recognition rate, compliance accuracy — before we move to the next phase.

  • 04

    Publisher tuning and compliance positions

    Publisher packs, metering logic, and licence entitlement models configured to produce defensible compliance positions for every in-scope vendor.

  • 05

    Operating model hand-back

    Trained internal team, documented governance cadence, and a sustainable operating model. We hand back. Your platform runs without us in the room.
